pkviewer has two ways of recognising people, and they are separate on purpose.

A **badge** appears on a system's public page. A **credit** appears on the [credits page](https://pkviewer.xyz/credits) and needs nothing at all — no pkviewer account, no PluralKit system. Someone who reports a security problem by email is credited without ever signing in.

You can have one, both, or neither.

### Badges

A badge is given by pkviewer. You cannot set one on yourself, and neither can anyone else — that is the entire point of having them.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Runs pkviewer. |
| **PK Dev** | Works on PluralKit itself. |
| **Girlfriend** | The owner's girlfriend. |
| **Friend** | A friend of the project. |
| **Bug Hunter** | Found and reported a bug. |
| **Security Researcher** | Reported a security vulnerability responsibly. |
| **Contributor** | Contributed to pkviewer. |

More can be added over time. The current list is always on [pkviewer.xyz/badges](https://pkviewer.xyz/badges).

!!!warning
The **PK Dev** badge recognises someone who works on PluralKit. It is pkviewer's way of saying who they are — it is not a statement by PluralKit, and it does not mean PluralKit is involved in pkviewer. pkviewer remains an independent third-party project, not affiliated with or endorsed by PluralKit.
!!!

### You decide whether it shows

Being given a badge does not put it on your page. It arrives as an offer, and nothing appears until you accept it.

That is deliberate. A badge says something about you to everyone who visits your page — a relationship, or that you found a security problem — and not everyone wants either of those made public. So:

- **Accept** and it appears on your system page.
- **Decline** and it does not. You can change your mind later.
- **Hide** one you accepted earlier. A hidden badge looks exactly like one that was never given; nobody can tell there is something to ask about.

You will find these on your system's management page.

**PK Dev is the one exception.** It appears without waiting, and it can be given to a PluralKit developer who has no pkviewer account at all — otherwise it would be a badge that never shows, and being recognised would mean signing up to a third-party site first. It is still yours to remove: claim your system and Decline works exactly as it does for any other badge, or [ask](/faq) and it will be taken off. Every other badge waits for you.

### Why a badge cannot be faked

Anything you write about yourself — your system name, your description, the label on a link — is ordinary text and is drawn as ordinary text. Badges are not part of your appearance settings, so a theme cannot restyle one, hide one, or make anything else on the page look like one.

Every real badge links to [pkviewer.xyz/badges](https://pkviewer.xyz/badges). If something looks like a badge and does not link there, it is not one.

### Credits

The [credits page](https://pkviewer.xyz/credits) lists people who helped: bug hunters, security researchers, contributors, testers, and anyone else worth thanking.

A credit is a name, what you did, and optionally a link you choose. It is not connected to any account, so being credited never requires you to sign up for anything. pkviewer shows the link and never visits it.

!!!secondary
Found a bug or a security problem? See the [FAQ](/faq) for where to send it. Security reports go by email, not a public issue.
