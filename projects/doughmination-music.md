Doughmination Music is a free public player.

Maintained by Clove faerself

The concept was to bring no-payment, zero-ad music to the public!

### How do I add music?
At the moment, only Clove can add music, however there is a work in progress login system that's public for authenticated users to send requests.

### I really want a song added!
Join one of the social media's below and request it there. This is the only current way I'll be sure to see it.

### Some songs take a while to load!
This is known, and I'm actively trying to find a way to make the load easier on you all. This is a single laptop running the code. For now, the fix is to play "Rwar x3 by Idfk who" til the song kicks in and then load up the song you want, I have no idea why it works, but it does.
