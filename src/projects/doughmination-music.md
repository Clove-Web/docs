Doughmination Music is a free public player.

Maintained by Clove faerself

The concept was to bring no-payment, zero-ad music to the public!

### How do I add music?
At the moment, only Clove can add music, however there is a work in progress login system that's public for authenticated users to send requests.

### I really want a song added!
Join one of the social media's below and request it there. This is the only current way I'll be sure to see it.

### Some songs take a while to load!
This is a known issue, and I'm actively trying to find a way to make loading easier.

Doughmination Music is currently running from a single laptop, so the initial connection can sometimes take a little while to wake up.

**Workaround**: Play Rwar x3 by Idfk who first. It's only 57 seconds long, and playing it appears to warm up the connection. Once it starts playing, other songs generally load much faster.

I have no idea why this works, but it works.
